> Living document. Tables are generated by `python -m pinca_jax.bench` and pasted
> from `results/bench_<pde>.md`. All numbers are **reduced-scale CPU** (no GPU);
> configs re-run unchanged on GPU. Every cell is **mean ± std over seeds** — no
> single-run results.

## Methodology
- **Task.** One-step autoregressive emulation of a PDE solver teacher. Each model
  `g_θ` is trained to match the teacher's `rollout_steps`-step rollout from random
  initial conditions (`ic.make_state`), then evaluated on a longer `eval_steps`
  horizon from held-out ICs (different PRNG stream) — testing temporal generalisation.
- **Shared protocol.** Identical teacher, ICs, horizon, optimiser (AdamW), and loss
  (rollout MSE) across architectures (`harness.py`). Differences are the architecture only.
- **Seeds.** ≥3 seeds; report mean ± std. Init, data, and dropout (none) all keyed off the seed.
- **Metrics** (`metrics.py`): rel-L2 (primary accuracy), MSE/RMSE, PSNR, **conservation
  error** (|mass(pred) − mass(IC)|), periodic-BC residual, gradient-energy (stability
  proxy), parameter count, training wall-clock, inference s/step.
- **Teacher stability.** Gray-Scott uses the stable `dt=1.0` teacher (verbatim `dt=2.0`
  diverges — see research log); all others use verbatim notebook params.
- **Scope honesty.** Reduced grids/horizons/epochs on CPU. Absolute numbers are not
  GPU-scale; *relative* architecture orderings are the object of study and are the part
  expected to transfer. `pmap`/sharding unused (single device).

## Reading guide / hypotheses under test
- **H1 (conservation).** `pi_nca` should show conservation error orders of magnitude
  below `plain_nca`/`fno` by construction. *(Already observed in smoke: ~1e-4 vs ~10¹.)*
- **H2 (locality vs global).** On purely local diffusion (heat), local NCAs should be
  competitive with or beat `fno` at far fewer params; on globally-coupled/stiff dynamics
  (Cahn-Hilliard, shallow-water) `fno`'s global mixing should help.
- **H3 (stability).** Long-horizon `grad_energy` blow-up indicates instability; conservation
  structure is expected to improve long-horizon robustness.
These are falsifiable and reported honestly whichever way they resolve.
